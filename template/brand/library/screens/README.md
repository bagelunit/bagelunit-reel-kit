# screens

Real screenshots and screen recordings that show up on screen as a phone or laptop display.

- **Formats:** PNG for screenshots; MP4 for short recordings (under 8 s).
- **Size limits:** screenshots under 3 MB; recordings under 30 MB.
- **Naming:** `where-what-01.png` (for example `app-booking-screen-01.png`).
- **What counts as real:** screenshots of the brand's own product or app, or of public pages you can see without logging in. Never another company's logged-in screen, a customer's account, or anything with names, emails, prices or numbers that are not cleared in `brand/facts.md`. Blur or replace personal details before saving.
- **Rights:** only things the brand owns or may use.
- Add a row to `../INDEX.md` for every file. The media files stay out of git; this README and the index stay in.
