# product-images

Photos and cut-outs of the brand's own products, packaging, team space or finished work.

- **Formats:** PNG (transparent cut-outs), JPG or WEBP (photos).
- **Size limits:** long side 2000 px at most, under 5 MB each.
- **Naming:** `product-angle-01.png` (for example `candle-front-01.png`).
- **Rights:** only things the brand owns or may use. Photos of people need that person's OK, and customer photos need the customer's OK. No client data, no photos of children.
- Add a row to `../INDEX.md` for every file. The images stay out of git; this README and the index stay in.
