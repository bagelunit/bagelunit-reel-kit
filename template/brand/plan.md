# plan.md: what this brand plans to post

One row per planned reel. `/content-calendar plan` proposes rows and adds them after your yes; you can also edit this table by hand. Date is the day you want it out (YYYY-MM-DD). Fill "Reel (folder)" once the reel is started (its folder name in `reels/`). Series is optional: reels with the same series name are grouped on the dashboard. `/content-calendar` shows these on the calendar and dashboard.
