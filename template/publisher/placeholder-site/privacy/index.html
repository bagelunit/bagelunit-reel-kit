{{BRAND}} publisher. Last updated {{DATE}}.

The {{BRAND}} publisher is a tool run by {{BRAND}} for its own use. It uploads {{BRAND}}'s own videos to {{BRAND}}'s own accounts. This page explains what it does with data.

What it accesses

When the {{BRAND}} team signs in to YouTube, the publisher asks Google for one permission: to upload videos to the {{BRAND}} YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It cannot read, change or delete the channel's existing videos, comments, subscribers or account details, and it does not ask for any other permission.

What it stores

It stores the sign-in token Google gives it, so it can upload videos later without asking again. The token is kept as an encrypted secret on {{BRAND}}'s own Cloudflare account and on the computer of the person who signed in. It also keeps the videos it is about to upload, and deletes each one once it has been posted.

Who it shares data with

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else. Videos and tokens go only to the platform they are meant for (for example, YouTube) and to the cloud services that run the tool (Cloudflare).

Viewers and visitors

The publisher collects no information about people who watch {{BRAND}}'s videos or visit this site. This site does not use cookies or analytics.

Google user data

The {{BRAND}} publisher's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube's Terms of Service and is bound by the Google Privacy Policy.

Taking access away

You can revoke the publisher's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that it can no longer upload to the channel.
